Two more researchers have joined the group: **Raziye Kartal** and **Ahmet Hüdai Başyemenici**.

Raziye joins us as an MSc student in the Bioinformatics and Systems Biology program at Gebze Technical University. Ahmet, a pharmacist by training, joins as an MSc student in the Artificial Intelligence program, bringing a background that bridges pharmaceutical sciences and machine learning.

We are glad to have them with us and look forward to the work ahead.
