We are excited to announce a new chapter authored by our researchers, published in the **Encyclopedia of Bioinformatics and Computational Biology (Second Edition)**.

### Computational Prediction of Immune Epitopes

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of computational methods for predicting immune epitopes. Epitope prediction plays a crucial role in vaccine design, immunotherapy development, and understanding immune responses to pathogens.

The chapter covers various computational approaches including sequence-based methods, structure-based predictions, and machine learning algorithms used to identify B-cell and T-cell epitopes. It discusses the strengths and limitations of different prediction tools and provides guidance for researchers working on immunoinformatics applications.

For more details, you can read the full chapter [here](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-323-95502-7.00268-2).
