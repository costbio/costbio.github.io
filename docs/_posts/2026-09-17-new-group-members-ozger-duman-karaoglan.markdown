Three new members have joined the group: **Duygu İrem Özger**, **Dicle Gülsüm Duman** and **Sude Karaoğlan**.

All three join us as MSc students in the Bioinformatics and Systems Biology program at Gebze Technical University. Duygu is a graduate of İstinye University, and Dicle joins us from Konya Food and Agriculture University. Sude is one of our own, a graduate of the Bioengineering Department here at GTU.

We are glad to have them with us and wish them many successes in the work ahead.
